ELC Accountability Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ELC Accountability Tracker ("ELC Tracker", "the app") is a private, invitation-only app for members of a financial agents' team to record their own business activity and for the team's leaders to follow the team's progress.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, title, and an encrypted password (we never see your password).
  • Activity you enter: daily invites, bookings, follow-ups, meetings, closed and cancelled clients, recruits, points, monthly targets and your selected gameplan.
  • Account information: your role (owner, admin or member), whether your account is active or deactivated, when you registered, verified your email and last signed in, and which invitation code you registered with.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use tracking or ads.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your dashboard, targets and progress.
  • To let the app's owner and administrators see your activity and progress (read-only) and manage accounts.
  • To send account emails (email verification and password reset).

Who can see your data

Only you, the app's owner and its administrators. Other members cannot see your data. Administrators can view your data but cannot change it. This is enforced by the database's access rules. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase (a managed PostgreSQL database), and protected in transit with HTTPS. Account emails are sent through our email provider.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. If your account is deactivated, you can't use the app but your data is kept so the account can be reactivated. When an account is deleted, the account and all of its data are permanently erased. A short record that the deletion happened (date, email address and who requested it) is kept for security purposes.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete my account, read the warning and type DELETE to confirm. Your account, sign-in, logged activity, monthly targets, gameplan history and team membership are deleted immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

Without the app: email prosperpath.team10@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my ELC Tracker account". We will delete the account and all of its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you only want to pause, ask your team leader to deactivate your account instead: your data is kept and the account can be reactivated later.

Your choices

You can view and edit your own activity and profile in the app at any time, and delete your account as described above.
